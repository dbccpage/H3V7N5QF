# Claim ledger, third derivation

Status: **given**, **chosen**, **derived**, **computed**, **refuted**, **open**
(see `paper/third/paper.md`). Lean names in namespace `Seed.Third` are in
`lean4/Third/`; names in `Seed.Second` are the second derivation's
(`lean4/Second/`), names in `Seed` the first's (`lean4/H3V7N5QF/`). Axiom
report: `evidence/third/lean_axioms.txt`; "std" means the theorem depends on
`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound` only; "none" means no axioms. No
`sorry`, `admit`, or declared axiom occurs anywhere in `lean4/` (scanned by
`code/third/leanreport.py`, recorded in the same file).

An ID written `1:…` is a claim of the first derivation (`paper/claim_ledger.md`),
one written `2:…` a claim of the second (`paper/second/claim_ledger.md`). The
column **Re-check** says how a cited earlier result was re-checked from the
third seed: *no change* (the statement mentions no change of state),
*embedding* (restricted through `Step2.toStep3` / `Reach2.toReach3`, and
through the second's `Step3.toStep` into the first's changes; the restriction
is itself a Lean theorem), *re-proved* (the earlier proof went through an
identification, or a stronger statement is proved afresh), *computed again*
(re-run from the third exploration and compared), or "—" for a claim new to
this derivation.
