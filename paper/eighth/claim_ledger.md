Status: **given**, **chosen**, **derived**, **computed**, **refuted**, **open**
(see `paper/eighth/paper.md`). Lean names in namespace `Seed.Eighth` are in
`lean4/Eighth/`; names in `Seed.Seventh`, `Seed.Sixth`, `Seed.Fifth`,
`Seed.Fourth`, `Seed` are the earlier derivations' (`lean4/Seventh/`,
`lean4/Sixth/`, `lean4/Fifth/`, `lean4/Fourth/`, `lean4/H3V7N5QF/`). Axiom
report: `evidence/eighth/lean_axioms.txt`; "std" means the theorem depends on
`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound` only (some on a subset); "none"
means no axioms. No `sorry`, `admit`, or declared axiom occurs anywhere in
`lean4/` (scanned by `code/eighth/leanreport.py`, recorded in the same file).

An ID written `1:…` … `7:…` is a claim of the first … seventh derivation. The
column **Re-check** says how a cited earlier result was re-checked from the
eighth seed and whether it *survives the second relation*, per relation,
jointly, or not: *no change* (the statement mentions no relation), *on a
part* (the statement holds for the `r` part and for the `s` part of a
two-relation state, each a state of the sixth seed, `DSt.proj`; "per
relation"), *through the embedding* (a state of the sixth seed as a
two-relation state asserting nothing with `s`, `MSt.ofR`, `Step6.ofR`,
`Reach6.ofR`; the statement is about a particular sequence and holds for it
here), *re-proved* (proved afresh for two relations; "jointly"), *computed
again* (re-run beside the eighth exploration and compared on the results
asserting nothing with `s`), or "—" for a claim new to this derivation.
