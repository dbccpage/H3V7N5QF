# Claim ledger, fifth derivation

Status: **given**, **chosen**, **derived**, **computed**, **refuted**, **open**
(see `paper/fifth/paper.md`). Lean names in namespace `Seed.Fifth` are in
`lean4/Fifth/`; names in `Seed.Fourth`, `Seed.Third`, `Seed.Second`, `Seed`
are the earlier derivations' (`lean4/Fourth/`, `lean4/Third/`,
`lean4/Second/`, `lean4/H3V7N5QF/`). Axiom report:
`evidence/fifth/lean_axioms.txt`; "std" means the theorem depends on
`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound` only (some on a subset); "none"
means no axioms. No `sorry`, `admit`, or declared axiom occurs anywhere in
`lean4/` (scanned by `code/fifth/leanreport.py`, recorded in the same file).

An ID written `1:…`, `2:…`, `3:…`, `4:…` is a claim of the first, second,
third or fourth derivation. The column **Re-check** says how a cited earlier
result was re-checked from the fifth seed and whether it *survives removal
lowering a multiplicity and identification merging terms*: *no change* (the
statement mentions no change, or only the state), *through the embedding*
(every change of the fourth seed is a change of the fifth, `Step4.toStep5`,
`Reach4.toReach5`; the statement is about the fourth's changes and holds for
the fifth's additions), *on supports* (the first derivation's statement is
the restriction to states asserting nothing twice, via `Step5.toStep`),
*re-proved* (proved afresh for the four kinds on counted states), *computed
again* (re-run beside the fifth exploration and compared on the results
containing what is given), or "—" for a claim new to this derivation.
"Survives" means the earlier statement holds as stated; "survives on the
fourth's results" means it holds exactly for states containing what is given;
"does not survive" means it is false under the fifth seed, with the witness.
