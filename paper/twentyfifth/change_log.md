# Change log, twenty-fifth derivation

Each material change, and its reason. Newest last. Commits are on the branch
`derivation/twentyfifth`; nothing is pushed.

1. **`CLAUDE.md` gained the twenty-fifth derivation's plan and stopping
   conditions (AN1–AN11), appended and committed alone before any other work.**
   Reason: required by `prompt/twentyfifth/prompt.md`. The text was checked
   against the prose watchlist before it was committed (clean), and `git diff`
   showed insertions only.

2. **Reading notes and scratch checks** (`research/twentyfifth/`): the seed's
   word diff; the ranks of the sums around the filled triples (three and four
   terms leave two directions beyond the loops unseen, five and six none); the
   sets of triples that decide with four and five terms; timings of the
   exploration. None of it is cited as evidence.

3. **Bounds raised above the plan.** The plan named 4 terms and 7 rounds, 3
   terms and 9, 2 terms and 24, and 5 terms and 7 if it completed. Every run
   completed in seconds, so the twenty-third's own bounds were used (5 and 8, 3
   and 10, 2 and 24), which lets its exploration be run again at the same bounds
   and compared result for result (D10). The whole evidence takes about seven
   minutes here.

4. **Layout: a new package `code/twentyfifth/`, launchers
   `code/run_twentyfifth.py` and `code/lean_report_twentyfifth.py`; no earlier
   module is modified.** Imported: the twenty-third's state helpers (`excess`,
   `normal_table`, `around`, `plain`, `nothing_certificate`, `result_key`), its
   cost and record, its exploration (for the comparison), its counts' listing of
   tables and two-term closed form, its seventh and first pairs, its statements;
   the ninth's changes and `perform`; the twenty-second's Lean report helpers;
   the writers and `compare_evidence`. Defined afresh: filled triples, the state
   carrying them, the five kinds, the marks, balance, the exploration with the
   fifth kind, told and blind, the keeping sets, the rank condition and its
   witnesses, the count by orbits of sets of triples, the embedding and the map
   back, reachability, the readings, the statements, and the checks
   (`FillingCheck`, sixteen disagreement lists).

5. **Lean: a library `Twentyfifth` in `lean4_core/`; the lakefile gains it as a
   library and its name at the end of the default targets** (recorded in
   `paper/twentysecond/change_log.md` item 15, the lakefile being the
   twenty-second's file). The name was appended to the existing list: the one
   line is changed by an addition only, nothing reordered or reworded.

6. **Lean: the targets closed on the first attempt, target 7 (rewriting)
   included.** Two proofs first depended on `Classical.choice`: an `omega` that
   proved an `iff` goal (`balanced_swap`, split into two directions), and a
   `Classical.byContradiction` in `not_told` (replaced by a case split on the
   decidable balance). Found by building the axiom report early, after each file.
   170 theorems in all.

7. **The axiom report also lists the 26 theorems of `Twentythird` and
   `Twentysecond` the paper and ledger cite as imported** (`#print axioms` for
   each, generated by `research/twentyfifth/make_axioms_file.py` from the
   backticked names). Reason: `ci/check_axiom_citations.py` requires every cited
   Lean name in this derivation's own report; the twenty-third avoided naming
   imported theorems, while here naming them lets the report show that each
   imported proof still rests on `propext` and `Quot.sound` only.

8. **The census of the twenty-third's records first grouped results by the list
   of the sizes of their least keeping sets, and so counted `a b; ara bsb` and
   `a b; ara` (kept by two single terms and by one) as different shapes.** The
   question is which terms keep a result from nothing, so results are now grouped
   by the *set* of sizes; the first record shared by results kept by sets of
   different sizes is then `(2, 0, 1)`, `a b; bsb` and `a b; bsa`.

9. **The small audit (`evidence.audit`) first stopped the readings at four
   rounds, one before reading C-I3's first cost failure, and reported a
   disagreement that was not there.** It now runs the readings to five rounds; the
   evidence runs them to six.

10. **`oriented_triples` was not sorted with four terms or more, so a state filled
    at every triple could not be built** (found by the first audit at four terms).
    It now returns the triples in order.

11. **`ci/check_mutation_bite.py` gained a twenty-fifth panel (sixteen bite cases,
    one per field of `FillingCheck`), added after the twenty-third's; the panel
    list gained one line.** Reason: required by the task. The audit behind it
    runs at three terms and six rounds, the smallest bound at which some result
    has several states and a filled triple, so the balance record check can bite.

12. **`ci/check_axiom_citations.py` lists the twenty-fifth derivation (one line
    added to the tuple); `ci/check_definitional_citations.py` gains the
    twenty-fifth's record noun (`balanc`), one entry added to its table.**
    Reason: so this derivation's citations are checked against its own report, and
    so a reflexivity theorem cited for a claim about balance must name balance.

13. **`ci/prose_watchlist.json` pins four hits of this paper — two outside-theory
    words the watchlist tags, twice each — all in its final section (§11,
    nearest known results).** Reason: the watchlist census counts them; the
    entry was added by hand with the digests `--probe` reports, as the
    twenty-second did (its item 11), since `--regen` would drop the pins of files
    this checkout omits.

14. **Every derivation's `--check` was re-run after the paper was written, all
    twenty-four of them, from `generate.py` to `run_twentyfifth.py`
    (`research/twentyfifth/check_earlier.py`). Each exited 0 and reported that its
    evidence matches a fresh regeneration.** Reason: stopping condition AN6. No
    earlier derivation's file was changed to correct a defect. The other gates of
    "Done" also passed on the committed tree: `pytest` (1 102 passed; 99.83 %
    line, 99.70 % branch; every evidence module fully covered), `mypy`, the prose
    watchlist, the vacuous-check, definitional-citation, mutation-bite and
    axiom-citation gates, and `lake +leanprover/lean4:v4.33.1 -d lean4_core
    build`, whose exit code was read on its own line as 0. The axiom report lists
    only `propext` and `Quot.sound`.
