# Claim ledger, fourth derivation

Status: **given**, **chosen**, **derived**, **computed**, **refuted**, **open**
(see `paper/fourth/paper.md`). Lean names in namespace `Seed.Fourth` are in
`lean4/Fourth/`; names in `Seed.Third`, `Seed.Second`, `Seed` are the earlier
derivations' (`lean4/Third/`, `lean4/Second/`, `lean4/H3V7N5QF/`). Axiom
report: `evidence/fourth/lean_axioms.txt`; "std" means the theorem depends on
`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound` only (some on a subset); "none"
means no axioms. No `sorry`, `admit`, or declared axiom occurs anywhere in
`lean4/` (scanned by `code/fourth/leanreport.py`, recorded in the same file).

An ID written `1:…`, `2:…`, `3:…` is a claim of the first, second or third
derivation (`paper/claim_ledger.md`, `paper/second/claim_ledger.md`,
`paper/third/claim_ledger.md`). The column **Re-check** says how a cited
earlier result was re-checked from the fourth seed and whether it *survives
the assertion being counted*: *no change* (the statement mentions no
multiplicity, or only whether an assertion is asserted — read as "positive
multiplicity"), *through the maps* (the earlier statement is the restriction
of the new one to states asserting nothing twice, via the support `toSt` and
the indicator `ofSt`; the restriction is itself a Lean theorem), *re-proved*
(a statement covering multiplicities is proved afresh), *computed again*
(re-run beside the fourth exploration and compared on the results asserting
nothing twice), or "—" for a claim new to this derivation. "Survives" means
the earlier statement holds as stated; "survives on simple states" means it
holds exactly for states asserting nothing twice and the counted form here
extends it; "does not survive" means it is false for counted states.
