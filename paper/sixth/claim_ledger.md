# Claim ledger, sixth derivation

Status: **given**, **chosen**, **derived**, **computed**, **refuted**, **open**
(see `paper/sixth/paper.md`). Lean names in namespace `Seed.Sixth` are in
`lean4/Sixth/`; names in `Seed.Fifth`, `Seed.Fourth`, `Seed.Third`,
`Seed.Second`, `Seed` are the earlier derivations' (`lean4/Fifth/`,
`lean4/Fourth/`, `lean4/Third/`, `lean4/Second/`, `lean4/H3V7N5QF/`). Axiom
report: `evidence/sixth/lean_axioms.txt`; "std" means the theorem depends on
`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound` only (some on a subset); "none"
means no axioms. No `sorry`, `admit`, or declared axiom occurs anywhere in
`lean4/` (scanned by `code/sixth/leanreport.py`, recorded in the same file).

An ID written `1:…` … `5:…` is a claim of the first … fifth derivation. The
column **Re-check** says how a cited earlier result was re-checked from the
sixth seed and whether it *survives once `xrx` is allowed*: *no change* (the
statement mentions neither legality nor a change), *through the embedding*
(every performed change and every attempt of the fifth seed is a change of
the sixth, `Step5.toStep6`, `Try5.toStep6`, `Reach5.toReach6`; the statement
is about a particular sequence or attempt and holds for it here), *on legal
states* (the earlier statement holds for the states asserting no `xrx` and is
used only there), *re-proved* (proved afresh for all well-formed states),
*computed again* (re-run beside the sixth exploration and compared on the
results asserting no `xrx`), or "—" for a claim new to this derivation.
"Survives" means the earlier statement holds as stated; "survives on legal
states" or "with the names apart" means it holds exactly there; "does not
survive" means it is false under the sixth seed, with the witness.
