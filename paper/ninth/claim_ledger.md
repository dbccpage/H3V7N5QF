An ID written `1:…` … `8:…` is a claim of the first … eighth derivation. The
column **Re-check** says how a cited earlier result was re-checked from the
ninth seed and whether it *survives with nothing given and two relations*:
*no change* (the statement mentions neither the given, nor the names, nor a
second relation), *through the maps* (an eighth state with its names
forgotten is a ninth state and every eighth change is a ninth change on the
forgotten states, `Step8.forget`, `Reach8.forget`; a ninth state with a
term, named, is an eighth state and every ninth change on it is an eighth
change on the named states, `Step9.name`, `Reach9.name`; a ninth state
projects to two seventh states, `Step9.proj`; a seventh state embeds as a
ninth state with nothing in `s`, `Step7.ofR`; the statement is about a
change or a sequence and holds for it here), *on a part* (the statement
holds for the `r` part and for the `s` part, each a state of the seventh),
*without object* (the statement is about the given, the names, `bra` or the
destroyed givens), *re-proved* (proved afresh for the ninth's states),
*computed again* (re-run beside the ninth exploration and compared on the
results asserting nothing with `s`, or on the results with a term), or "—"
for a claim new to this derivation. "Survives" means the earlier statement
holds as stated; "survives as …" means the shape holds after the restatement
named; "does not survive" means it is false under the ninth seed, with the
witness.
