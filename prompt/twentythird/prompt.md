Begin now from `prompt/twentythird/seed_prompt.md`. It is the twenty-second seed with its sameness sentence extended and one sentence added: two results that differ only by raising terms are now also the same result, and raising a term is defined. Determine what follows and what does not.

In the twenty-second, can a result with something asserted be the same as a result with nothing asserted on as many terms; and here, which results are, whether each other result carries something that shows it is not, what the fewest changes to each result are, and which of its states assert the fewest assertions?

Explore by performing changes on states, as the twenty-second's exploration does, and form the results afterwards.

Throughout:

1. record for every notion whether it was given, chosen, derived, computed, refuted, or left open;
2. state what does not follow;
3. prove every claimed necessity and every claimed impossibility;
4. keep every non-isomorphic alternative unless a proof eliminates it, and keep every reading of the seed that gives a different result;
5. where you use a result of an earlier derivation, cite it and check that its proof still holds from this seed, and say in the ledger's re-check column how: no change, re-proved, imported, or computed again.

Relating this seed to the earlier ones, in Lean:

- Choose an earlier seed in this one's line (the twenty-second, the ninth, the seventh, or the first) whose states map one to one into this seed's results, with each of its changes carried to a change here. If none does, prove that with a witness.
