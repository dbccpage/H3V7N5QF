# Input

Glyphs, in codepoint order: `<` `>` `a` `|`

Nothing about the glyphs or their arrangement is given.

The candidates are every arrangement of zero to five glyphs, with repetition. Generate them; list none by hand.

For each candidate, determine what follows and what does not. Declare what counts as a change, and record every change. Attempt 100 changes along each alternative, finishing earlier changes on every alternative before later ones. Do not invent a change to reach 100. If the alternatives exceed what can be completed, declare a finite bound, export the exact frontier, and prove what is complete within it.

Any outcome is valid.
