## Task

This repository holds fifteen derivations: a first (`prompt/seed_prompt.md`, `paper/paper.md`), a second through a fifteenth (`prompt/second/` … `prompt/fifteenth/`, `paper/second/` … `paper/fifteenth/`). Each strikes or adds one sentence of the change calculus and derives what follows.

Begin now from `prompt/sixteenth/seed_prompt.md`. Determine what follows and what does not. This seed is the fourteenth (`prompt/fourteenth/`), which recorded, after every change, a leftover written with a third letter `ℓ` and *inert* — "a leftover is not an assertion and is not an answer". The sixteenth changes exactly that one sentence: **a recorded leftover feeds back into the state.** A leftover `xℓy` may now be admitted, adding the assertion `xry` or the assertion `xsy`; only an unadmitted leftover is not an assertion. The leftover names no relation (the fourteenth's finding), so a leftover left by a removal in one relation may be admitted into either.

The question of this seed is what the feedback does. In the tenth through fifteenth the record sat *off* the result, so it was not a third multiplicity, not an assertion, not a change — true by construction. Here the record re-enters the state, so those are no longer free: determine whether removing an assertion and then admitting its leftover to the *same* relation is the identity, and to the *other* relation is a genuinely new result; whether the feedback terminates or a leftover can be admitted and re-removed without bound (declare a finite bound and export the frontier if so); what the cost of a result is when an assertion can arrive either by a direct addition or by admitting a leftover; what reachability, the result set, and the count become; and which of the earlier derivations' results survive the feedback and which fail.

Throughout:

1. record for every notion whether it was given, chosen, derived, computed, refuted, or left open;
2. state what does not follow;
3. prove every claimed necessity and every claimed impossibility, by exhaustive enumeration over a declared finite candidate space or by a Lean theorem — never by prose;
4. keep every non-isomorphic alternative unless a proof eliminates it; quotient only under an implemented isomorphism;
5. where you use a result of an earlier derivation, cite it and check that its proof still holds from the sixteenth seed — in particular, a claim that held *by construction* because the record sat off the result must be re-examined now that the record feeds back, and downgraded from "derived" to "chosen"/"by construction", or re-proved, as the evidence requires.

Do not steer toward a known theory. The final section of the sixteenth paper names the nearest known results and how the findings differ. The prose watchlist (`ci/check_prose_watchlist.py`) pins outside-theory terms to that final section; keep it clean.

Use only this repository. No network, other files, or questions. Do not modify `prompt/`. Change an earlier derivation's files only to correct a defect, and record each such change in the relevant `change_log.md`.

First add your plan and stopping conditions for the sixteenth derivation to `CLAUDE.md`, and commit them.
