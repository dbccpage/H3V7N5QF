# Fenced review and proposal area

**Chosen boundary:** `meta/` analyzes the completed derivations and proposes
future seeds. Its categorical, biological, and Genesis vocabulary is not an
input to any derivation, and nothing here authorizes a claim in a paper.
Each proposed seed lives here until separately commissioned; `prompt/` is
unchanged. Confirmed, refuted, computed, and open correspondences are scoped
in `immune_system/`, while `review/` holds the audit and local receipts.

**What does not follow:** a claim in `meta/` is not an inherited theorem.
For example, `Third/Cost.lean` proves exact step size for its `Step2`, while
the proposed removal in `next_ten/01-remove-bare-term/` has only a bounded
prototype. Both are legal objects to discuss here, with different evidence
statuses. A future derivation must start from its own seed and perform its
rule-5 re-checks.

**Fence for the climbing track (author's ruling of 2026-09-25, D1).**
`climbing/` holds the rubric, the frozen extension class and one packet per
rung. Every climbing seed is chosen by an operator with a target in view.
So no paper and no file here may claim that this repository reached any
outside structure independently, and climbing results are graded "blind
(operator-targeted)". The vocabulary and comparison rows of the outside
level table that motivates the track stay out of this repository, with the
table's owner. Only digests of its key terms are watched here. Each rung's
seed and task use the parent seed's words; a run's worktree omits `meta/`.
`climbing/rulings.md` records the author's six rulings of that date.
