## What does not follow

The local detector chronology does not prove a decline in defects. A later
definitional-citation gate *did* expose three further 13–15 rows; the same
family of gates *did not* expose the seventeenth's missing parent axiom
receipts before this review. Those are two legal historical witnesses for
local detection and a remaining miss; `defect_incidence.md` gives their
counts and different review granularities. A standardized future cohort
would be needed to estimate a rate.

Likewise, a recorded leftover does not always change reachability. In the
tenth–fifteenth chosen state models the ninth's state and steps are inherited
by identity, while the sixteenth's admitted leftover can add an assertion.
These two legal constructions delimit the feedback claim. A general
evolutionary or categorical theory of the whole repository remains **open**.
