# Prose watchlist decision for the fenced analysis

**Chosen:** the categorical terms `category`, `categories`, `functor`, and
`morphism`, the biological terms `immune`, `antibody`, `antibodies`,
`Darwinian`, and `Lamarckian`, and the external repository name `Genesis`
should join the digest watchlist.
They are used explicitly in `meta/` to analyze the derivations, but a future
blind derivation paper should have to pin any use deliberately. `Genesis` is
included because the same name could otherwise import an outside framework
into a blind paper body under the guise of source attribution. The gate's
documented plural stems do not turn `categories` into `category` or
`antibodies` into `antibody`, so both spellings need their own digest. The
eight initial probes and two follow-up probes found none of these words
watched before each addition. The `--add` and `--regen` commands, and the
before/after pin comparison,
are recorded in `meta/review_log.md` when the independent meta branch is
prepared.

**What does not follow:** a clean count does not show that a paper avoids
outside influence. A future author can write an equivalent idea without a
watched word; the present `meta/immune_system/` files openly contain the
watched words and are valid because they are fenced analysis. These are two
legal writings with different relationships to the seal. The gate is a
deliberate vocabulary census, not a semantic proof.
